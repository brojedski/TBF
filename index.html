Not sure where to begin?

A simple way to use the hubs

  1. Choose one hub

    New to remote work? Start with FreelancingHub. Already working and curious about AI? Go to AI Hub.

  2. Do one lesson a day

    Small and steady beats cramming. Most lessons fit into a coffee break.

  3. Use the tools right away

    Price yourself with the rate calculator, draft a proposal, check a job post for scams. Learn by doing.

  4. Come back often

    New lessons, platforms and tools get added regularly, so there's always something fresh.

You don't need to have it all figured out to start. You just need to start, then keep going. That's how all of us got here.
